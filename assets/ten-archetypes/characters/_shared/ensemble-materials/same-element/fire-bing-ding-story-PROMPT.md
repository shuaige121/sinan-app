## Prompt

```text
Create one original horizontal story illustration for Changming City using the supplied two character images as strict identity references and the face-study image as the exact drawing-style reference.

Preserve both humans independently:
- Left/foreground: 丙·忘归, the adult man from image 1, same lively brown curls, bright eyes, broad smile, sculptural brass earrings, charcoal-brown modern jacket, ivory knit, dark trousers, healthy open build.
- Right/midground at a shaded doorway: 丁·西窗, the adult woman from image 2, same chin-length dark bob and mature quiet face, smoke-gray cropped jacket, deep wine knit, dark trousers, slender contained build, protecting the same small plain egg-shaped amber fire-seed lamp between both hands.

Scene and meaning: a communal workshop courtyard at first light, with practical work bays and lamps for many people. Forgetting no one, 忘归 steps into the open courtyard with arms spread naturally, distributing attention and morning warmth outward. The rising sunlight and two broad cloud arcs create only a subtle visual echo of an unfolded phoenix or sun—no literal bird, wings, feather costume, magical aura, or central sacred furnace. 西窗 remains at the boundary between the dim lamp-keeping room and the courtyard, sheltering the tiny oval amber seed. Her light is visibly weaker, but it preserves the possibility that the broad fire can return. Their eye lines acknowledge each other without romance posing. The same fire is shown in two states: unfolded public warmth and enclosed future hope.

Open-ended moment: dawn is beginning but not completed; some lamps remain dark, work is still underway, no victory tableau, no final balance, no text. Human, restrained, emotionally alive. Clean Japanese editorial manga ink, restrained flat cel shading, slightly imperfect hand-drawn line, warm cream paper texture, graphic readable silhouettes, similar refinement to the supplied face-study—not polished AI fantasy art. 16:9 horizontal composition, both full figures visible, ample environment, no captions, no symbols, no logo.
```

## 使用边界

- 凤凰与卵是火势“展开／收拢”的同源状态，不是物种、血统或服装主题。
- 不生成实体凤凰、破壳幼鸟、羽毛披风、火焰翅膀或奇幻法器。
- 不把五合、同部自动画成恋爱关系。
- 画面停在正在交接的时刻，不给城市永久平衡或复燃完成的视觉结论。
